# 成品参考与原稿审计

本 skill 的骨架来自五条外部参考稿（2026-07-31 收到）。下面先是审计结论，再是按契约修订后的五条成品——可直接复制生图，也是新主题的对照样本。

---

## 一 · 原稿审计（重整操作）

### A 会直接画坏物件的四项

| # | 问题 | 出现在 | 后果 | 修法 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | `laptop sits fully visible` 与 `the laptop and desk edge cropped by the bottom edge of the frame` 自相矛盾 | 1、2、4 | 模型二选一，出现半截键盘或双层机身 | 裁切唯一：只裁桌面，笔记本整机在画内并留出下边距 |
| A2 | 桌子从未作为实体存在，只在 Composition 里以 "desk edge" 出现 | 全部五条（2 最严重：笔记本 "beside them" 完全悬空） | 笔记本浮空、尺度失控、机身插进人体 | Scene 段固定尾句写实桌面；Subject 写明底座压在桌上并带接触阴影 |
| A3 | 同一台笔记本要求「机身网点 + 屏幕扁平」，却走 `three-quarter angle`，且未说明屏幕 UI 在边框内部、共享透视 | 全部五条 | 屏幕脱离机身飘着、屏幕面与机身透视不一致、第二块屏 | 单体连续 + 屏幕在框内 + 改浅角度近正面 |
| A4 | 手部超预算 | 1（扶眼镜 + 触控板）、2（抱臂同时单手持纸，姿势自相矛盾）、4（双手捧杯）、5（手臂勾椅背 + 指向画外） | 多指少指、第三只手、手臂与椅子融合；「指向画外」会让模型凭空造物 | 手部预算：一只手做动作，不碰脸不包握；视线必须有画内落点 |

### B 版面算术超额

原稿：标题块 33% + 人物 32% + 卡片区约 30% ≈ 95%，同时还要求 generous negative space 和左右留白——加不到 100。人物 32% 在 3840px 宽画面上是 1230px，实际会把留白吃光。

修法：改成能加总到 100% 的四带预算，人物压到 24%。见 `../SKILL.md` 的版面预算表。

### C 配色角色冲突三项

| # | 问题 | 出现在 | 修法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | swash 是 `pale warm grey`，一块中性灰压在人物后面，与本条自带的 `no large flat featureless grey plane` 直接打架 | 3 | 改 pale dusty lilac；立规则：swash 必须低饱和有色相 |
| C2 | `warm sand` 同时是背景色与占位条色名 | 1 | 占位条改 warm greige |
| C3 | `no cool or blue-toned greys` 会连带把 navy / dusty blue / teal 一起洗淡 | 全部五条 | 改写为「中性灰保持暖调，色板自带的饱和蓝/青/紫/绿不受影响」 |
| C4 | `clear cream margin above the upper card`，但背景是 warm sand——cream 是别的色板漏过来的 | 1 | 统一由 Composition 基座说「background 余量」，不再点色名 |

### D 其余五项

- chip「压在两卡之间的角上」未指明哪个角（五条全部）→ 固定为跨压上卡左下角。
- 六笔涂鸦未给落点区 → 固定三处：chip 附近、标题与人物之间、swash 上方。
- 屏幕上的 token chips 可能被写成真单词 → Text 段与 Constraints 双重封堵。
- 人物着装与裁切完全未定，成组产出会每张漂 → 由人物调度表接管。
- 五条的 Constraints 几乎逐字相同 → 抽成 Constraints 基座，成组产出时逐条照抄。

### E 判定为有效、原样保留的

原稿这几处是对的，不动：纹理作用域三分（人物/椅子/机身 vs 屏幕/卡片/背景/有机形状）、网点通透条款（不糊成黑块、不成均匀灰）、卡片 1px 暖灰细边 + 紧贴低透明度投影、两卡不同构、有机形状 <18% 且限于底角、`render all text verbatim` 条款、`no long curved arrows spanning across the frame`、accent 不上植物线。

Only the desk surface is cropped by the bottom edge of the frame; the whole laptop stays inside the frame with clear space between its base and the bottom edge. The organic shapes stay in the bottom corners under 18% of the frame height and sit behind everything else.

Text in image: a small all-caps kicker "CRAFT NOTES" in deep ink navy, and directly beneath it a large bold black headline "WRITING FOR MACHINES" set in two lines in a heavy geometric sans-serif, together occupying the left third; three card labels only: "Prompt", "Drafts", "v3". Every other piece of copy, on the cards and on the laptop screen alike, is rendered as an abstract placeholder bar with no letterforms, except for single-letter or numeric column labels. Render all text verbatim, no extra words.

Constraints: the laptop is one continuous object — no detached or floating screen, no screen panel separated from its base, no second screen, no duplicated or mirrored laptop, no laptop clipping into or overlapping the person's body, and no part of the laptop cropped by any edge of the frame; no extra or missing fingers, no third arm, no hand touching the face, no hand passing through the desk or the laptop; no readable words or sentences on the laptop screen, single letters and numerals used as column labels being the only lettering allowed there; no halftone texture on the laptop screen, the cards, the desk or the background; no white outline around the laptop, desk, chair or any furniture; no photographic colour on the person, strictly black-and-white halftone; no large flat featureless grey plane anywhere; no large soft blurry drop shadows on the cards; no identical structure between the two cards; no long curved arrows spanning across the frame between cards; no accent colour on the botanical line accents; all neutral greys stay warm and never cool or blue-toned, while the palette's own saturated blue, teal, plum or green hues are unaffected; no gradients on the background; no 3D effects, no glassmorphism, no texture on the cards or background; the headline must not overlap the person, the laptop or any card; preserve the flat vector cleanliness of every UI card; no real brand logos, no watermarks.
