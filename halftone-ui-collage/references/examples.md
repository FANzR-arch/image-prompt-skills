# 正确构图示例

下面的示例只演示正确的人物与电脑关系：人物在前景坐姿，屏幕从肩后出现，键盘鼠标在显示器前方的桌面上。

```text
Create a 5:2 editorial collage banner. Left title: kicker "WEEKLY DATA", headline "WEEKLY\nRETRO". A black-and-white halftone analyst sits in the foreground on an office chair, body slightly turned toward the viewer; their face and upper body stay fully visible. A landscape desktop monitor with thin bezel, real stand and visible base sits on the rear desk behind their right shoulder; a keyboard and mouse rest flat in front of it. Its screen shows a flat metrics dashboard made only of placeholder bars. Right cards: a line chart with one burnt-rust circled peak and a metrics list, chip "REVIEW". Pale linen background, deep espresso-brown bottom waves, pale seafoam swash, deep teal details, burnt-rust highlights. The person, chair, monitor shell and keyboard are halftone; screen, cards and desk are flat. No laptop, tablet, floating screen, missing stand, screen blocking the person, duplicate device, readable screen text, logo or watermark.
```

检查顺序：先看人物是否在显示器前方；再看显示器是否从肩后露出、是否有支架和底座；最后才看卡片、网点与配色。
