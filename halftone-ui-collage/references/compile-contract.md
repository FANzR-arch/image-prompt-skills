# 编译契约（桌面工作站版）

每条提示词都使用下列四段。不得删除或改写“人物前景 + 肩后显示器”的关系为笔记本前景构图。

## 1. 工作站关系（每条必带）

```text
PERSON AND WORKSTATION — one person, a black-and-white fine-halftone photographic cutout with restrained abstract dither, scattered ink-speckle noise and a soft grainy transition inside the figure silhouette; keep facial features clear. Use a white sticker outline around the person only. The person is seated in the foreground on an office chair, body turned slightly toward the viewer. Their face and upper body are fully visible. Behind or diagonally behind them, on the rear part of a real desk, is one unbranded landscape desktop monitor visible over one shoulder. The monitor has a thin bezel, a real support stand and a visible base resting on the desk; a keyboard and mouse lie flat on the desk directly in front of it. The monitor is part of the person's believable workspace, not a floating screen, foreground product, laptop, tablet, or screen blocking the person.
```

## 2. 版式骨架（每条必带）

```text
A 5:2 wide editorial collage banner. TEXT — kicker "<KICKER>" and large two-line headline "<HEADLINE>" in the left third. Follow the user's title language exactly: Chinese input uses Chinese; English input uses English. Within one image, kicker, headline and card labels must use one language only; never mix Chinese and English. Card labels "<LABEL_A>", "<LABEL_B>", "<CHIP>". Render these words verbatim and add no other readable copy.

SCENE — flat <BG> background with no gradient; small <INK> dot-grid clusters in both upper corners; <ORGANIC> organic waves in both bottom corners with fine botanical line accents in the same tone. A real desk runs across the lower scene. A pale <SWASH> brush swash sits behind the person.

UI — the monitor screen is a flat clean vector interface for <SCREEN>, with abstract placeholder bars and no readable words. In the right third, stack two white rounded cards with a visible gap: <GRAPH_CARD> and <LIST_CARD>; add one small rounded <ACCENT> chip overlapping the upper card corner.

STYLE — halftone texture applies only to the person, chair, monitor outer shell and keyboard; the monitor screen and UI cards remain perfectly flat and clean. Add subtle low-contrast warm paper fibre and printed-ink grain to the background, desk and organic shapes for tactile editorial depth, never affecting legibility. Use <INK> for icons, numbers, kicker and doodles; <ACCENT> only for status accents; <GREY> for placeholder bars, hairlines and borders. Crisp hairlines, tight low card shadows, at most six asymmetric doodle marks.

CONSTRAINTS — one seated person only; one desktop monitor only; no laptop, tablet, detached or floating screen, missing support stand, impossible desk contact, second display, duplicate keyboard, monitor blocking the face, person standing beside the computer, person placed behind the monitor, extra or missing fingers, halftone on screen/cards/desk/background, white outline around furniture, photographic colour on the person, gradients, 3D effects, glassmorphism, logos or watermarks.
```

## 3. 失败判断

- 显示器跑到人物前面、挡住脸或像独立产品：失败。
- 屏幕没有支架与底座，或键盘、鼠标与显示器不在同一张桌面：失败。
- 出现笔记本、平板、第二块屏或悬浮 UI 屏：失败。
- 任何家具带白色贴纸描边，或屏幕/卡片/桌面带网点：失败。

## 4. 调试顺序

先锁定空间关系，再锁显示器支架与桌面接触，最后补 UI、色彩和涂鸦。不要用更多设备形容词修补错误构图。
