# 成品参考 · 已验证提示词

首批四条来自长文《FDE 是临时外包 or 真 AI 落地？》配图，四条共用同一套风格基座与色板，是**成组产出**的标准样例。

装配方式：把下面任意一条的 **Subject** 段和 **Text in image** 段整段替换，Scene / Key details / Composition / Constraints 逐字不动，即可换一张图。

**出图参数**：size `2560×1440`，quality `high`

---

## 完整成品（直接可用 · 装置「流程 / 阶段」）

其余成品只需替换 Subject 与 Text 两段。

```text
Use case: article illustration, wide 16:9 explainer graphic.

Scene: pure flat white background, no environment, no horizon, no gradient; one thin black horizontal ground line running the full width beneath the blocks.

Subject: five flat rounded-square blocks of equal height stand side by side in a single row along the ground line, in order from left to right coloured deep blue, yellow, green, orange and pink; each block carries one simple thin white line icon on its face — a handshake, a screen, a magnifying glass over a chart, a wrench, and a circular arrow with a clock; a stick figure with a thick uniform black outline, round head and no facial features strides forward on top of the row, mid-step between the third and fourth block, both arms swinging naturally.

Key details: flat vector illustration; all figures drawn with thick uniform black outlines, round heads, no facial features, and left completely unfilled white inside; all blocks are flat saturated solid colour with absolutely no gradient, no shading and no texture, drawn from the palette deep blue, orange, green, purple, yellow and pink; icons inside the blocks are simple thin white line drawings; one soft pale grey ellipse shadow directly under each standing figure and nothing else casts shadow; generous empty white space between elements; mood: clean, friendly, instructional, modern explainer.

Composition: 16:9 horizontal frame; the row of five blocks occupies the lower half and spans the full width with margins at both edges, the walking figure sits above the row slightly right of centre; the entire upper third of the frame stays empty clean white.

Text in image: five small labels in black — Latin lowercase sans-serif, Chinese in a clean Chinese sans (思源黑体 / Source Han Sans) only, one centred under each block from left to right: "lead", "demo", "validate", "deliver", "maintain". Render all text verbatim, no extra words.

Constraints: no gradients, no drop shadows other than the pale grey ellipse under the figure, no outlines on the colour blocks, no facial features on the figure, no additional text, labels, logos or watermarks, no photographic elements, no 3D rendering, no background colour of any kind.
```

## 已验证 Subject 句（换这一段即换一张图）

### 1 · 装置「从 A 到 B / 派驻」

> on the left, a simple stick figure with a thick uniform black outline, round head and no facial features, steps out of a flat blue rounded-square building block marked with a small white outline icon of a company tower; a thin black line with a small arrowhead arcs across the empty middle of the frame to the right side, where a flat orange rounded-square block marked with a small white outline icon of a desk represents the destination; a second identical black-outline stick figure sits cross-legged directly beside that orange block, leaning slightly forward as if listening; three small flat green squares and two small flat purple squares float loosely along the connecting line.

配套 Composition 微调：`the left figure and blue block occupy the left third, the orange block and seated figure occupy the right third, the connecting line and floating squares cross the empty centre; all elements sit within the middle band leaving clear white margins at top and bottom.`

配套 Text：两个标签，分别在两个色块下方。

---

### 2 · 装置「理想 vs 现实」

本条是这套风格最有张力的一个装置，对比全靠**排列秩序**而非颜色：左边等距对齐，右边角度杂乱互相重叠。

> on the left, a flat blue rounded-square screen block stands upright on a small stand, its face carrying three tidy evenly spaced white horizontal bars and one small white upward arrow, everything neat and aligned; in the centre, a tall flat yellow vertical wall block rises from the ground line to two thirds of the frame height, blocking the way; on the right, behind the wall, a dense messy cluster of small flat squares in green, purple, orange and pink tilts at many different angles, overlapped and misaligned, with several thin black lines tangling between them and two short black lines ending in dead-end dots; a stick figure with a thick uniform black outline, round head and no facial features stands in front of the wall on the left side, one arm raised toward the wall, head turned to look up at it.

配套 Key details 追加：`the left screen reads as orderly and evenly spaced while the right cluster reads as chaotic and overlapping;`

配套 Composition 微调：`the tidy blue screen occupies the left third, the yellow wall stands at the exact centre, the chaotic cluster fills the right third and spills slightly beyond the wall's top edge; clear white margin across the top third of the frame.`

---

### 3 · 装置「一进多出 / 产出有两份」

分岔的两条线**必须等权重**，否则读者会以为其中一条是主线。

> at the centre, a stick figure with a thick uniform black outline, round head and no facial features stands facing forward holding a flat purple rounded-square block with both hands, a small white gear icon on its face; from behind the figure a single thin black line runs in from the left frame edge; from the block, two thin black lines with small arrowheads split apart and travel outward — the upper line rises to the right toward a flat orange rounded-square block bearing a small white outline icon of a running system with a checkmark, the lower line descends to the right toward a flat green rounded-square block bearing a small white outline icon of stacked reusable templates; four small flat green and purple squares float loosely along the two diverging lines.

配套 Key details 追加：`the two output lines are clearly symmetrical in weight so neither reads as more important;`

配套 Composition 微调：`the figure with the purple block sits slightly left of centre in the lower half, the two diverging lines open toward the right third where the orange and green blocks sit stacked vertically apart; clear white margin across the top third.`

---

### 4 · 装置「分类 / 清单 / 构成」

对应大对话气泡内部分格，每格一图标一标签。适合"这件事由 N 个部分组成"。

> a very large speech bubble with a thick uniform black outline occupies the right two thirds of the frame, its interior pure white and divided by thin black horizontal lines into four equal rows; each row holds one flat colour icon on the left of its label — a deep blue person silhouette, an orange target with an arrow in the centre, an orange heart with a white star, and a deep blue shield with a white star; a stick figure with a thick uniform black outline, round head and no facial features sits cross-legged on the lower left, one hand raised to the chin in a thinking pose; two thin curved black lines run from the figure's head toward the bubble, with a loose scatter of small flat green and purple squares riding along them.

配套 Composition 微调：`the seated figure occupies the lower left quarter, the speech bubble fills the right two thirds; the scattered squares bridge the empty space between them.`

配套 Text：每行一个标签：英文小写无衬线，中文用思源黑体这类干净黑体（不写 lowercase），贴在图标下方或右侧。

---

### 5 · 装置「协作 / 谈判 / 师徒」（草稿，未出图验证）

人物本身就是装置主体，不配色块。姿态和朝向差异是唯一的角色区分手段——正对/侧对、坐/站、手势朝向谁。

> two stick figures with thick uniform black outlines, round heads, no facial features and completely unfilled white interiors, stand facing each other at the centre of the frame on the same ground line; the left figure stands upright with one arm extended forward, palm open; the right figure sits on a small flat green rounded-square block, leaning slightly forward with both hands resting on its knees; a single thin black line runs from the left figure's open palm to the seated figure, ending in a small arrowhead; a loose scatter of three small flat orange squares floats above the connecting line.

配套 Key details 追加：`the two figures differ only in posture and orientation, never in outline weight or fill;`

配套 Composition 微调：`the standing figure occupies the left third, the seated figure and green block occupy the right third, the connecting line crosses the empty centre; clear white margin across the top half.`

配套 Text：两个标签，分别贴在两个人物脚下的地面线附近。

> 使用前先按七槽实际编译出图验证一次，跑通后再补进「已验证」区。

## 5:2 超宽变体 · 仓库/分节横幅

画幅拉到 5:2 之后横向空得多，**色块必须减到三个以内**，否则重心散掉。人物退到左三分之一当发起者，右三分之一放结果，中间整片留白让线穿过。

本条用于 `Phil-design-skills` 仓库封面。**出图参数**：size `2560×1024`（精确 5:2；长边 <3840、双边 16 的倍数、比例 ≤3:1、总像素 655,360–8,294,400），quality `high`；1500×600 直接等比缩放即可，不要裁切。

```text
Use case: GitHub repository banner, ultra-wide 5:2 format.

Scene: pure flat white background, no environment, no horizon, no gradient; a thin black horizontal ground line only beneath the figure.

Subject: on the left third, a stick figure with a thick uniform black outline, round head and no facial features stands facing right with one arm raised; a single thin black line leaves the raised hand, travels right across the empty centre of the frame and forks into three branches, each ending in a small arrowhead; the three branches reach three flat rounded-square canvas blocks of equal size standing in a staggered vertical arrangement in the right third — the upper block deep blue carrying a simple white line icon of a portrait frame, the middle block orange carrying a simple white line icon of a poster with one large letter, the lower block green carrying a simple white line icon of a small node diagram; a loose scatter of small flat purple squares rides along the three forking branches.

Key details: flat vector illustration; all figures drawn with thick uniform black outlines, round heads, no facial features, and left completely unfilled white inside; all blocks are flat saturated solid colour with absolutely no gradient, no shading and no texture, drawn from the palette deep blue, orange, green and purple; icons inside the blocks are simple thin white line drawings; one soft pale grey ellipse shadow directly under the standing figure and nothing else casts shadow; the three branches are equal in line weight so no single output reads as the main one; generous empty white space between elements; mood: clean, friendly, instructional, modern explainer.

Composition: 5:2 ultra-wide horizontal banner; the figure occupies the left third standing on the ground line, the three canvas blocks occupy the right third staggered vertically around the horizontal centre line, the forking lines cross the empty middle third; clear white margins at the top and bottom edges and at least half the total frame stays empty clean white.

Text in image: one small lowercase sans-serif label in black, placed directly beneath the figure: "Phil-design-skills". Render all text verbatim, no extra words.

Constraints: no gradients, no drop shadows other than the pale grey ellipse under the figure, no outlines on the colour blocks, no facial features on the figure, no additional text, labels, logos or watermarks, no photographic elements, no 3D rendering, no background colour of any kind.
```

装置选型：仓库是一组风格编译器，讲的关系是**一进多出**——一个想法进去，多种风格出来。三个画布块各自的图标（人像框 / 大字海报 / 节点图）对应仓库里三类风格 skill，读者不用看说明就知道这个仓库是干什么的。

## 成组产出检查表

一组图发出去之前逐条过：

- [ ] 每一条正文里都完整写了 Scene / Key details / Constraints 三段基座（不是靠"同上"）
- [ ] 每一条的 palette 从句一字不差
- [ ] 一组内没有两张讲同一个关系
- [ ] 一组内人物姿态有变化（站 / 走 / 坐 / 抬手），不要四张都是同一个站姿
- [ ] 单张色块 ≤5 个、颜色 ≤4 种
- [ ] 标签全组统一语言、统一大小写风格

## 常见塌方与修法

| 症状 | 原因 | 修法 |
|---|---|---|
| 色块带了黑边，像剪贴画 | Constraints 里漏了 `no outlines on the colour blocks` | 补回该句，别靠改描述绕 |
| 人物被填成灰色或黑色 | Key details 没写 `left completely unfilled white inside` | 补该句 |
| 出现五官、表情 | 漏了 `no facial features` | 在 Key details 和 Constraints 两处都写 |
| 画面发闷、密不透气 | 色块超过五个或颜色超过四种 | 删元素，不要靠加留白描述补救 |
| 色块下方冒出没要求的英文 | 没加 verbatim 条款 | 补 `render all text verbatim, no extra words` |
| 出现投影、立体感 | 模型自动加了 drop shadow | Constraints 明写只允许人物脚下的浅灰椭圆 |
