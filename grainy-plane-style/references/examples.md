# 从零生成 · 六条参照示例

覆盖矩阵：主体六类不重、配色六种不重、机位六种不重。固定块已对齐当前 DNA（主体带颗粒、背景零噪），可直接取用。

**未逐条实测**——DNA 固定块本身在照片改风格模式里已验证，这六条是按母版编译的组合参照，出图偏差先怀疑 Subject 槽的点名，不要动固定块。

## 示例 1｜打字机局部（器物 · P3 暗调单点 · C5 极特写）

```text
Use case: editorial illustration still life, vertical 2:3, no text.

Scene: a single flat dark moss-green field that is perfectly smooth and
completely free of any grain, noise or texture, no scenery, no horizon.

Subject: an extreme close-up of part of an old typewriter — three rows of round
keys cut off by the frame edges, the curved platen roller crossing the upper
area as one broad plane, a single sheet of paper rising behind it, and one
vermilion return lever as the only bright element; built from only a handful of
large planes.

Key details: the subject only is filled with a dense, fine, even stipple, like
spray paint pushed through a stencil, and that stipple erodes the subject's
boundaries into finely speckled, slightly ragged edges — each shape stays
completely legible and in focus, yet no edge is a smooth clean vector line. The
background stays untouched clean flat colour with no grain at all. Keep tonal
steps minimal: one even colour per plane, no faceted shading inside a plane.
Closed palette — dark moss green field, iron grey and slate grey for the machine
planes, cream for the paper, vermilion for the single lever, near-black for the
key faces.

Composition: extreme close-up from a slight high angle, the machine filling most
of the frame and cropped on three sides, one wedge of empty green field in the
upper left corner.

Text in image: no text.

Constraints: no grain, noise or texture anywhere in the background, no smooth
clean vector edges, no flat crisp digital shapes, no faceted tonal stepping, no
blurring or soft focus, no woolly or felted texture, no low-poly triangulation,
not photorealistic, no 3D render, no outlines, no gradients, no logos, no
watermark.
```

## 示例 3｜峭壁灯塔（地景 · P5 冷灰加一点暖 · C6 极远景）

地景是唯一允许放宽单一色场的题材：两条平涂色带，两条都须写零噪。

```text
Use case: editorial illustration landscape, vertical 2:3, no text.

Scene: two flat colour bands only — a large misty grey-blue sky field above, a
deep teal sea field below, meeting at a clean horizon; both bands perfectly
smooth and completely free of any grain, noise or texture; no rendered scenery,
clouds or waves.

Subject: a small lone lighthouse on a low charcoal headland at the right, built
from only a few large planes — a tapering limestone-white tower, a charcoal cap,
and one small warm-orange square of lit window; the headland reads as a single
dark wedge dropping into the sea.

Key details: the subject only is filled with a dense, fine, even stipple, like
spray paint pushed through a stencil, and that stipple erodes the subject's
boundaries into finely speckled, slightly ragged edges — each shape stays
completely legible and in focus, yet no edge is a smooth clean vector line. The
sky and sea bands stay untouched clean flat colour with no grain at all. Keep
tonal steps minimal: one even colour per plane, no faceted shading inside a
plane. Closed palette — misty grey-blue sky, deep teal sea, charcoal headland,
limestone white tower, one warm orange accent.

Composition: extreme wide view from eye level far out at sea; the lighthouse
occupies only a small part of the lower right, the horizon sits low in the
bottom third, and the sky field fills the upper two thirds as open space.

Text in image: no text.

Constraints: no grain, noise or texture anywhere in the sky or sea bands, no
smooth clean vector edges, no flat crisp digital shapes, no faceted tonal
stepping, no blurring or soft focus, no woolly or felted texture, no low-poly
triangulation, not photorealistic, no 3D render, no outlines, no gradients, no
logos, no watermark.
```

⚠️ 本条踩了组合禁忌 1（C6 极远景 × 细密颗粒）：灯塔太小时颗粒会盖掉形状。若出图主体糊成一团，把灯塔放大到最小色块占画面 3% 以上，或整条改用 C4 平视中景。
