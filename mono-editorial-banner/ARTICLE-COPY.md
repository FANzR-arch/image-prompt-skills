# 黑白编辑横幅 · 单文件分发版

整段复制下面的内容发给 AI（ChatGPT / Claude 均可），只填【主题或判断】和【标题语言】，其余不要改。

出图时用 5:2 画幅、尽量高的分辨率（推荐 3200×1280）和最高质量档——这套风格的发丝衬线和细线地面都吃分辨率。

---

请根据我给出的指令，生成一张「黑白编辑横幅」的配图提示词。

输入：
【主题或判断：】
【标题语言：中文 / 英文】

生成规则：

1. 只输出一段提示词，不要解释、不要给选项、不要反问。
2. 按七段结构输出并保留段落标签：Use case / Scene / Subject / Key details / Composition / Text in image / Constraints。正文一律英文，唯一例外是要渲染进画面的标题文字。
3. 主题只决定 Subject 段的装置内容与 Text 段的两行文字；**Scene、Key details、Composition、Constraints 四段逐字照抄下面的固定块，一个词都不改**。
4. 画幅锁死 5:2，已写在固定块里，不要改动。
5. **先判断这句话讲的是什么关系，再从下列清单里选一个装置写进 Subject，不要自创、也不要每次都用第一项**：①表面光鲜 vs 底下代价 → 悬浮的洁白屏幕，正下方一团黑色缠结线缆 ②反复修补才成立 → 单一黑色块体，带焊缝、缺角、打磨痕 ③看不见的东西在承重 → 白色平面 + 沉入纯黑的桥墩或地基 ④深不见底的积累 → 白色抽屉拉开，内部是无底黑洞 ⑤连锁反应 → 一列黑色多米诺排向消失点 ⑥方向与判断 → 单一黑色精密仪器（罗盘 / 天平 / 卡尺 / 摆锤）⑦门槛 vs 真正的差异 → 白色机械手指向一个孤立黑球 ⑧被选中 → 白色堆叠中唯一一片被抽出悬浮的黑 ⑨复利与长期 → 巨大黑色飞轮 + 一只白袖手部搭在轮缘 ⑩信号 vs 噪音 → 密集黑点场中一个孤立实心体 ⑪入口收窄 → 白色漏斗下方汇成一股黑色细流 ⑫断裂不可逆 → 一根横梁断成两截，断口锋利。
6. **Subject 段必须明确点出三件事**：这个装置叫什么名字（具体实物，不能是抽象几何形）／**哪一块是纯黑**（画面唯一的黑色锚点）／**只有哪一处受光**（其余沉入黑或白）。缺一项就重写。
7. Text in image 段只渲染两行：上方小字 descriptor，下方超大主标题。**两行都用衬线体**。英文用 Didone 衬线（极端粗细对比 + 发丝衬线），descriptor ≤3 词、主标题 ≤4 词；中文用高对比宋体（横细竖粗 + 尖锐三角衬角），descriptor ≤6 字、主标题 ≤8 字。段尾必须带 verbatim 条款。
8. 选中文时，Text 段末尾加一句 `Render every Chinese character exactly as given, with correct and complete strokes; no invented, malformed or extra glyphs.`，并在 Constraints 末尾追加 `no malformed or invented Chinese characters, no Latin text anywhere in the image.`
9. 语言留空按英文处理；主题留空时，按「一个被长期忽视但真正承重的底层系统」生成默认文案与装置。

固定块（逐字照抄）：

Scene: pure white void; a sparse floor of hairline black perspective lines appears only in the lower third and fades quickly toward the horizon; no other environment.

Key details: strict black-and-white palette with extreme tonal purity — the background reads as clean paper white, the black anchor reads as solid ink black, grey exists only as a narrow transition on the subject's own surfaces and never as a field tone; clean studio product-photography lighting with one soft directional key light; one clear black anchor mass carries the composition while everything else dissolves toward white or into black rather than competing for light; hard clean edges throughout; photorealistic rendering; mood: minimal, rational, quietly surreal, premium editorial.

Composition: 5:2 horizontal banner; headline centered in the upper 30% of the frame; the subject occupies the lower half; at least half of the total image area stays empty clean white.

Constraints: no overall grey cast, no mottled grey noise, no film grain, no colour of any kind; no additional text, labels, logos or watermarks; no second competing subject and no scattered props; the subject never overlaps or occludes the text.

Text 段写法（按语言二选一，填入两行文字后整段输出）：

英文 —— Text in image: two lines only, centered, rendered as flat solid-black graphic type with no shadow, no bevel, no perspective. Line 1: "［descriptor］" in small, widely letterspaced uppercase serif. Line 2: "［主标题］" in very large high-contrast Didone serif with extreme thick-thin stroke contrast and hairline serifs, the first visual focus; size ratio about 1:6. Render all text verbatim, no extra words.

中文 —— Text in image: two lines only, centered, rendered as flat solid-black graphic type with no shadow, no bevel, no perspective. Line 1: "［descriptor］" in small, widely letterspaced Chinese Song/Ming serif characters. Line 2: "［主标题］" in a very large high-contrast Chinese Song/Ming serif typeface with thin horizontal strokes, thick vertical strokes and sharp triangular serifs, the first visual focus; size ratio about 1:5. Render every Chinese character exactly as given, with correct and complete strokes; no invented, malformed or extra glyphs.

Subject 段的写法要求：以一个具体实物名词起手，写清它的状态、材质和投影，明确指出哪一块是纯黑锚点、只有哪一处受光；需要时补一句锐点点名（例如 sharp points to preserve: the extended fingertip, the sphere's edge）。装置若含屏幕或纸面，Constraints 末尾追加 `any small text on the screen or paper stays blurred and unreadable`。
