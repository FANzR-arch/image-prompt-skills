# 成品示例：四种图面模式各一张

寄存器：CYANOTYPE（深蓝底白线）。质感档：PRINT-02 STANDARD。四张共享同一套线型分级、同一段 PRINT TEXTURE 和同一套标题栏字段名——这是成系列的关键。

`PRINT TEXTURE` 段在四条里**一字不差重复**，改档要四条一起改。

---

## 封面（5:2）— TOPOLOGY 流程拓扑

> 带大标题的封面必带「标题图文分离铁律」四条（净空+绕行 / 实心 vs 线稿 / 字重分层 / 底板 knockout），见 compile-contract.md。下面是应用后的成品。

```text
STYLE ANCHOR — a real engineering drawing sheet: a process topology diagram drafted to standard, printed as a deep-blue cyanotype.

A 5:2 landscape sheet.

PAPER — a deep Prussian-blue cyanotype sheet; every line, glyph and label is printed in white drafting ink; flat printed line-art with no photographic depth, no gradient fill, no glow.

LINEWORK — a strict drafting line hierarchy: thick solid outlines for the node circles and the sheet border, medium solid lines for the edges between nodes, hairline dimension lines with arrowheads at both ends and short extension lines, thin long-short dash-dot center lines through the main horizontal axis, and thin evenly dashed hidden lines for the one broken connection.

LAYOUT — a wide horizontal clear band is reserved across the center for the title; the topology is routed so its edges sweep in two arcs, one above and one below this central band, and never cross behind the title.

DOMINANT VISUAL — one wide node-and-edge topology drawn as a drafting diagram: a single ENTRY node on the far left fans out to five thick-outlined circular nodes N1 to N5 whose edges arc over and under the central title band, converge into a MERGE node on the right and continue to one EXIT node; each node holds a small line-icon (gear, chart, target, cylinder, wrench); small solid dots mark the junctions and arrowheads terminate each edge; two leader lines with arrowheads annotate individual nodes, reading PROCESS NODE and ACTION NODE.

TITLE TREATMENT — the main title sits centered on a knockout plate in the paper blue with generous padding and a thin ruled white border that interrupts any line behind it; rendered very large and dominating the composition in solid heavy white ink with fully filled letterforms — the only solid mass on a sheet of otherwise hollow linework.

DRAFTING APPARATUS — a ruled border with zone letters down the sides and zone numbers along the top and bottom, kept faint; a ruled title block in the lower right with every cell filled (PROJECT / TYPE / SCALE 1:1 / SHEET 01); a legend box in the upper right keying NODE / EDGE / FLOW; a vertical scale bar with tick marks along the left edge and a horizontal one along the bottom; small registration crosses at the four corners — all kept lighter so they never compete with the title.

TYPOGRAPHY — Latin labels, codes and figures in technical drafting lettering, uppercase, evenly spaced; any Chinese title set in a heavy condensed Chinese sans (思源黑体 / Source Han Sans, Heavy) with solid filled strokes.

TEXT — main title must render exactly: 先画图，再动手. The title block cells read PROJECT / FLOW-01, TYPE / TOPOLOGY, SCALE / 1:1, SHEET / 01 — every cell carries a value, none left blank. All other text is short Latin field labels and figures only: ENTRY, N1, N2, N3, N4, N5, MERGE, EXIT, PROCESS NODE, ACTION NODE, NODE, EDGE, FLOW; no random dates, URLs, fake brands, full sentences, QR codes, or real code.

PRINT TEXTURE — the whole frame is a photograph of a real drafting sheet. Fine paper tooth runs throughout, crossed by faint horizontal press-roller banding at even spacing. Ink density is uneven, with lighter patches where the print washed out and heavier pooling along some lines. Two soft fold creases run through the sheet, catching a little light along their ridges. The edges fade, the corners darken, and a few specks of dust and fibre sit on the surface. Every line, letter and dimension figure stays sharp and fully legible through the texture.

AVOID — any colour beyond blueprint blue and white ink; neon green, phosphor glow, bloom, scanlines or any screen texture; uniform line weight with no hierarchy; empty unfilled title-block cells; lines crossing behind the title; title rendered as hollow outline type; photographic objects or depth of field inside the drawing itself; 3D rendering; glossy sheen; a visible desk, hands, drafting tools or room around the sheet; dense unreadable blocks of technical prose.

QUALITY — authentic cyanotype blueprint print, crisp white linework holding up through the paper texture, precise drafting alignment, real paper tooth.
```

## 换纸张寄存器怎么改

只动 `PAPER` 和 `QUALITY` 两段，再改 AVOID 里那句颜色约束，其余一字不动：

- **WHITEPRINT**：两段换成 compile-contract 的 WHITEPRINT 预设，AVOID 首句改成 `any colour beyond off-white paper and indigo ink`。标注多、字多的图纸走这个更好读。
- **SEPIA**：换成 SEPIA 预设，AVOID 首句改成 `any colour beyond tan paper and sepia-brown ink`。配 PRINT-03 是档案感最足的组合。

## 换质感强度怎么改

只动 `PRINT TEXTURE` 一段，四条一起换：

- 标注太密、字被吃 → 整段换成 **PRINT-01 CLEAN**。
- 要旧、要档案感 → 整段换成 **PRINT-03 AGED**，并在 AVOID 末尾补一句 `stains, creases or handwriting strong enough to obscure any line or figure`。注意 PRINT-03 会引入红笔批注，那是唯一允许的第二色。

不要封面上重档、插图上轻档——同一篇里质感档不一致，缩略图并排一眼就散。
